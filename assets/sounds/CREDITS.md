# Sound credits

Real animal recordings in this folder (`cow`, `dog`, `cat`, `duck`, `frog`, `horse`, `owl`, `pig`, `rooster`,
`sheep`) come from [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com/) by Joseph SARDIN, released under **CC0**
(public domain; free for any use, including monetized YouTube videos, no attribution required).
They were taken from the bundle in [andlo/ovos-skill-sound-like](https://github.com/andlo/ovos-skill-sound-like)
(see its CREDITS.md for the original BigSoundBank pages) and trimmed to the main call.

Sounds not listed here (bird tweet, car honk and engine, bus horn, dino roar, intro jingle) are synthesized by
`engine/sfx.py` at render time.

To add or replace a sound, drop a CC0/public-domain `.wav` named after the sound key into this folder;
the engine prefers a file here over its synthesized version.
